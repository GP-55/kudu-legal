PART A — GENERAL (all users; South African / POPIA baseline)

1. Summary (the short version)

  • We collect the information you give us (your account details, fitness profile, workouts, body metrics, injury/pain flags, photos, and messages to the AI Coach) and a small amount of technical data needed to run the app.
  • Much of this is health-related information, which POPIA treats as "special personal information" and the GDPR treats as a special category. We process it only with your consent and to provide the Service to you.
  • We use it to run the app, personalise your training, power the optional AI Coach, keep the Service secure, and comply with the law. We do not sell your personal information.
  • To provide the AI Coach, relevant parts of your messages and training context are sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to generate a response.
  • You have strong rights over your information, including access, correction, deletion, and withdrawing consent. Contact [PRIVACY EMAIL].

2. Who is responsible for your information

[LEGAL ENTITY NAME] is the responsible party (POPIA) / controller (GDPR) for your personal information — meaning we decide how and why it is processed. Where we act only on another party's instructions we will say so.

3. The personal information we collect

Fields marked (health) are, or may be, special personal information / special-category health data.

a) Account and identity data — email address; display name and/or username; profile photo/avatar if provided; authentication data managed by our backend provider (e.g. a hashed password / session tokens). We never see your password in plain text.

b) Fitness profile and onboarding data — gender (health), height, body weight (health), unit preference; experience/training level, training goal, training days per week and weekdays, session-duration preference, available/excluded equipment, and whether you opt into 1RM testing.

c) Workout and performance data (much of this is health-related) — workout sessions you log (date/time, duration, exercises, sets, reps, weights, distance, time, RPE, estimated calories, notes); personal bests, estimated and tested 1-rep-max values, progression history, weekly compliance, streaks, and the plans you start, adapt, or complete; free-text notes on exercises or workouts.

d) Body-metric and health-signal data — special personal information (health) — body-weight check-ins over time and related notes; exercise feedback and pain/injury flags (e.g. whether an exercise causes you pain, and injury indicators such as back-pain or knee-pain flags used to tailor plans). This is sensitive health data and we treat it accordingly.

e) Photos — profile pictures and any workout/celebration photos you choose to add, plus the camera/photo-library permission you grant. Photos may reveal health-related information and are treated with care.

f) AI Coach data — the messages you send to and receive from the AI Coach (your conversation history); the context we assemble to answer you (relevant parts of your profile and training history); and technical usage records of each request (e.g. model used and token counts) used for cost control, rate-limiting, and abuse prevention (these do not contain message text but are linked to your account).

g) Subscription and entitlement data — whether you have an active entitlement to paid features, its source and expiry. Payment card details are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play, not by us. We may receive limited transaction-confirmation data from the stores. Our subscription infrastructure provider (RevenueCat — Section 7) receives your account identifier together with the store's purchase, renewal, and expiry data so that your entitlement can be recognised on any device you sign in on.

h) Technical and device data — basic technical data needed to operate and secure the app (app version, device/OS type, language, diagnostic/crash information, and network metadata such as IP address at the time of a request, used transiently for security and delivery).

We do not intentionally collect data beyond what is described here. Please do not send us, or type into the AI Coach, sensitive information you do not want processed as described in this Policy.

4. How we collect it

  • Directly from you — when you register, complete onboarding, log workouts, add notes or photos, set goals, or chat with the AI Coach.
  • Automatically — a limited amount of technical/diagnostic data generated when you use the app.
  • We do not buy personal information about you from third-party data brokers.
